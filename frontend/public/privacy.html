Privacy and removal

Updated 19 September 2026 · AncientLens community pilot

AncientLens is operated by its solo founder. Contact ancientlens.contact@gmail.com for support, access, correction, rights or removal requests. The operator monitors this inbox. Browsing is free and does not require an account.

What the service stores

Accounts store a username, a salted password hash, hashed session and recovery tokens, and operational timestamps. Choose a pseudonym if preferred. We do not ask for an account email address. Sign-in uses an essential session cookie valid for up to seven days. There are no advertising or analytics cookies in this application.

Submissions store translation text, author credit, sources, rights evidence, uncertainty, AI-assistance disclosure, immutable revision history and terms acceptance. Reports, moderation decisions and removal requests are retained to operate the service and resolve disputes. Do not submit passwords, private contact details or other sensitive personal information in these fields.

Saved artifacts and private notes

My Library stores saved artifact identifiers, your private notes (up to 4,000 characters each), edit versions and timestamps. These are accessible only to your account through the application, not to other contributors or moderators. The service operator can access the database for operation and support; notes are not end-to-end encrypted. Export your records from My Library. Up to 100 saved artifact records are supported.

Removing a saved record clears its current note. Account closure clears current saved records. Minimal deletion identifiers and timestamps remain to replay removals into restored backups. Historical note versions may remain in encrypted backups until normal retention expires; removal is not immediate erasure of all copies. Private notes are not published or licensed as contributions.

Visibility and reuse

Pending drafts are visible only to their contributor and moderators. Accepted original-work revisions may become public after moderation under CC BY-SA 4.0. Permission-supported material remains private pending a separate rights-clearance process. Moderation does not establish scholarly accuracy. Public material can be copied by others under its applicable license.

Withdrawal and account closure

Withdrawal immediately removes public visibility for all revisions of your contribution. Account closure withdraws your contributions and revokes access. These controls request erasure; they do not immediately delete private history or backup copies. The operator reviews removal requests and can purge scoped catalog records. Minimal identifiers and completion records may remain to prevent withdrawn content from returning during restoration. Contact the operator to ask about a request or material in someone else's submission.

Lawfully reused copies held by other people cannot be recalled by removing them here. Private correspondence sent to the support inbox is handled separately from the application database.

Hosting, security and retention

The pilot runs on Oracle hosting in Paris with encrypted Backblaze recovery copies in its European endpoint. Network providers process connection metadata to deliver the service. The application uses hashed short-lived sign-in throttling records. Routine web access logs are disabled in the deployment configuration; operational and security logs may still contain connection metadata.

Recovery copies use a 30-day operational retention setting. Successful maintenance verifies a fresh downloaded restore before retiring expired copies or copies predating completed catalog erasure. Expiry depends on maintenance succeeding, and provider version cleanup is asynchronous. This is not a promise of immediate deletion from every copy. Account and contribution records otherwise remain until withdrawal/closure and operator review; no automatic catalog-purge deadline is implemented.

If both your password and recovery code are lost, automatic account recovery is unavailable. Never send either to the support inbox.
